## 2. Evidence Summary

1. **Migration 045 (`supabase/migrations/045_bind_invitation_rpc.sql`):**
   - Created `bind_invitation_to_user(p_invitation_id UUID, p_user_id UUID) RETURNS JSONB`.
   - `SECURITY DEFINER` with fixed `SET search_path = public, pg_catalog`.
   - Enforces `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` row-level exclusive lock on `user_invitations` to prevent concurrent acceptance races.
   - Validates existence, pending status, non-expiration, no previous binding (`accepted_by IS NULL`), and no conflicting profile.
   - Atomically creates `public.profiles` row and updates invitation status to `accepted` (`accepted_by = p_user_id`, `accepted_at = NOW()`).
   - Privilege boundary strictly enforced: `REVOKE EXECUTE FROM PUBLIC, anon, authenticated` and `GRANT EXECUTE TO service_role`.
   - Zero caller-controlled role or tenant parameters: `role`, `tenant_id`, `email`, and `full_name` are extracted exclusively from the locked invitation row.

2. **Server-Side Callback Sync Hardening (`src/lib/auth/callback-sync.ts`):**

   - Documents the unused `token` column (`REC-0014`).
