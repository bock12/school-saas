## 2. Mandatory Supervisory Amendments Addressed

1. **Authorization Model Separation (Amendment 1):** RLS policies strictly separate tenant boundary checking (`tenant_id = public.get_user_tenant_id()`) from role authorization (`public.is_school_admin()`, `public.is_super_admin()`, etc.).
2. **Table-Specific Exam Authorization (Amendment 2):** Distinct policies created for `exam_sessions`, `exam_schedules`, `exam_results_approval`, `exam_malpractices`, and `exam_appeals`. Privileged workflows (`results_approval`, `malpractices`) are sealed against unauthorized tenant users.
3. **Read-Only Analytics (Amendment 3):** The 6 exam analytics tables are treated as derived reporting snapshots, granting read-only access to authorized staff while reserving mutations for `super_admin` / service processes.
4. **Notification Recipient Ownership (Amendment 4):** Implemented `public.get_user_recipient_notification_ids()` (`SECURITY DEFINER`, `SET row_security = off`) to prevent infinite recursion while enforcing that users can only select and update their own notifications.
5. **Profile Mutation Trigger (Amendment 5 & 6):** Implemented `trg_protect_profile_mutations` ensuring non-super_admin / non-service_role users cannot modify `role`, `tenant_id`, or `is_active`. Fully preserves `bind_invitation_to_user` RPC provisioning. PROFILE-01 through PROFILE-07 tests all pass.
6. **Hardened Database Helpers (Amendment 7 & 8):** `get_user_tenant_id()`, `is_super_admin()`, `is_school_admin()`, `is_org_admin()`, `is_teacher()` all enforce `is_active = true` with `SECURITY DEFINER`, `SET search_path = public`, and `SET row_security = off`. Deactivated users fail closed.
7. **Authentic Non-Service-Role RLS Test Harness (Amendment 9 & 10):** `tests/security/rls-database-boundary.test.ts` sets `SET LOCAL role = 'authenticated'` and PostgreSQL session settings `request.jwt.claim.sub` and `request.jwt.claim.role`. Zero service-role queries were used to claim RLS enforcement.
8. **Explicit T-015A through T-015E Tests (Amendment 12):** Tested and passing.
9. **`public.tenants` Insecure Policy Removal (Amendment 13):** Dropped `Prototype allow all` (`USING (true) WITH CHECK (true)`).
10. **Application & Build Integrity (Amendment 14 & 15):** TypeScript typecheck (`npx tsc --noEmit`) and production build (`npm run build`) completed with zero errors.
11. **Security Control Matrix & Findings (Amendment 16 & 17):** Updated `.ai/06-MODULES/SECURITY-CONTROL-MATRIX.md` with layer separation and classified findings RLS-001 through RLS-007.
12. **Scope Boundaries (Amendment 18):** No new roles invented; role redesign deferred to TASK-0007.
