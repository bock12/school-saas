## 2. Core Architectural Recommendation

Gemini submits for supervisory approval ADR-0003: Contextual Functional Assignment Architecture (.ai/02-ARCHITECTURE/DECISIONS.md), formally rejecting both flat single-role limitations and combinatorial role explosion (teacher_hod_form_master):

```text
User Identity (auth.users)
      ↓
Tenant Membership (public.profiles.tenant_id + active status)
      ↓
Base System Role (public.profiles.role: super_admin, org_admin, school_admin, teacher, student, parent)
      +
Functional Assignment(s) (HOD, Form Master, Subject Teacher, Exam Officer)
      ↓
Effective Permissions (<module>.<resource>.<action>)
      ↓
Resource Scope (platform | org | school | department | class | offering | self)
      ↓
Deterministic Authorization Decision (ALLOW / DENY)
```

- Database Enum: Remains immutable with 6 canonical base roles (super_admin, org_admin, school_admin, teacher, student, parent).
- Functional Appointments: Reconciles the Exam Officer disconnect via dedicated relational appointment table public.school_exam_officers (tenant_id, teacher_id, is_active). Leverages existing relational anchors:
  - HOD: departments.head_teacher_id
  - Form Master: sections.class_teacher_id
  - Subject Teacher: teacher_assignments (teacher_id, section_id, subject_id)
- Separation of Duties: Enforces strict multi-stage workflows for examination marks (Teacher enters -> HOD moderates -> Exam Officer validates -> Principal approves -> Principal publishes) and curriculum lifecycle.
